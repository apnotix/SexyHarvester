# 🌿 SexyHarvester

![WoW Forever](https://img.shields.io/badge/WoW-Forever-e8c26a?style=for-the-badge) ![Retail](https://img.shields.io/badge/WoW-Retail-c8a24a?style=for-the-badge) ![Interface](https://img.shields.io/badge/Interface-120100%20%7C%2016001-3b2b12?style=for-the-badge) ![Edit Mode](https://img.shields.io/badge/Edit%20Mode-yes-4fc16a?style=for-the-badge)

**Everything you gather, at a glance.**

Shows the herbs, ore, leather and cloth in your bags with their count and a green **+N** for what you gathered this session. The window appears by itself as soon as you carry materials.

---

## ✨ Features

- 🔎 **No setup needed:** materials are detected automatically, new ones work right away.
- ➕ **Session counter:** green **+N** per material, total as a badge in the title.
- 🪟 **Three views:** compact, as a row, or with names grouped by profession.
- 🛠️ **Edit Mode:** position, size, layout, growth direction, background, border and font, all in Blizzard's Edit Mode.
- 🎨 **Look** matching **SexyLoot** and **SexyQuestHelper**.
- 🌍 **English and German.**

## ⌨️ Commands

- `/sh reset` - reset the session counter
- `/sh` - list all commands
